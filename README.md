# 📊 Cognifyz Data Science Internship Tasks – 2024

**Where Data Meets Intelligence**

Welcome to the official repository for the **Cognifyz Data Science Internship Program – 2024**. This internship provided a hands-on opportunity to explore, analyze, and model real-world data while completing structured tasks across three levels. This repository contains all the tasks I completed during my internship.

---

## 🧠 Internship Overview

Participants were required to complete any **2 out of the 3 levels**:  
- **Level 1**: Data Exploration and Basic Analysis  
- **Level 2**: Feature Engineering and Advanced Analysis  
- **Level 3**: Predictive Modeling and Data Visualization  

> Completing **Level 3** (2 out of 4 tasks) made candidates eligible for a **stipend**.

---

## ✅ Completed Task List

### 🟢 Level 1 – Beginner

1. **Data Exploration and Preprocessing**
   - Explore dataset dimensions (rows and columns)
   - Handle missing values and convert data types
   - Analyze distribution and imbalance of the target variable (`Aggregate rating`)

2. **Descriptive Analysis**
   - Compute statistical measures (mean, median, std, etc.)
   - Analyze distributions of `Country Code`, `City`, and `Cuisines`
   - Identify top cuisines and cities

3. **Geospatial Analysis**
   - Visualize restaurant locations using latitude & longitude
   - Analyze restaurant distribution across cities/countries
   - Explore correlation between location and rating

---

### 🟡 Level 2 – Intermediate

1. **Table Booking & Online Delivery Analysis**
   - % of restaurants offering table booking and online delivery
   - Compare ratings with/without table booking
   - Online delivery availability across price ranges

2. **Price Range Analysis**
   - Most common price range
   - Average rating per price range
   - Color representation with highest average rating

3. **Feature Engineering**
   - Create new features from restaurant name/address
   - Encode variables like "Has Table Booking" or "Online Delivery"

---

### 🔴 Level 3 – Advanced

1. **Predictive Modeling**
   - Build regression models to predict `Aggregate Rating`
   - Train-test split, evaluate models (Linear Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forest)
   - Compare model performances

2. **Customer Preference Analysis**
   - Relationship between cuisine types and ratings
   - Popular cuisines based on votes
   - Cuisines receiving higher average ratings

3. **Data Visualization**
   - Charts showing distribution of ratings
   - Compare average ratings by cuisines and cities
   - Explore feature relationships visually
